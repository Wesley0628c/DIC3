## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: ICEB Conference Paper Manuscript
The submission SHALL provide `ICEB paper/main.txt` containing a complete IEEE conference format paper with ICEB header metadata, integrity notes, and modular section references.
#### Scenario: Inspect ICEB paper file
- **WHEN** the reviewer reads `ICEB paper/main.txt`
- **THEN** the ICEB 2027 header, integrity boundaries, and IEEE document environment are present.
