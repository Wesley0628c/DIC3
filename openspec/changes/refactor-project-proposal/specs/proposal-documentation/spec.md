## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: Research outline
The submission SHALL contain Abstract A–F, five core mindsets, three trade-offs and ten design details for Food Guardian.
#### Scenario: Review the outline
- **WHEN** the reviewer opens docs/proposal-strengthening.md
- **THEN** all six abstract elements and the 5/3/10 sections are available.

### Requirement: Refactored research chapters
The submission SHALL provide Introduction and Related Work with traceable references and a comparison table.
#### Scenario: Inspect related work
- **WHEN** the reviewer reads the related-work chapter
- **THEN** AI technology, domain knowledge, research limitations and the project gap are distinguished.

### Requirement: Proposal evidence
The submission SHALL describe evaluation plans as expected work and SHALL NOT present unexecuted research as measured findings.
#### Scenario: Planned experiment
- **WHEN** a proposed dataset size or metric is stated
- **THEN** it is labelled as planned rather than an obtained result.

### Requirement: Design-to-spec mapping
The submission SHALL connect DESIGN requirements and workflow to OpenSpec requirements and scenarios.
#### Scenario: Trace cancellation behavior
- **WHEN** the reviewer follows the confirmed-update design
- **THEN** a cancellation scenario specifies that inventory is unchanged.
