# Paper References Specification
## Purpose
Define requirements for BibTeX bibliography curation and IEEEtran citation style compliance.

## Requirements
### Requirement: BibTeX bibliography curation
The submission SHALL maintain standard BibTeX entries matching all in-text citations.
#### Scenario: Inspect bibliography
- **WHEN** the reviewer inspects paper/references.bib and paper/sections/006_references.tex
- **THEN** all cited keys are defined with verified metadata.
